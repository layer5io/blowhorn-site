One message. Many ears

Blowhorn is a social media console that takes one message and broadcasts, reposts and amplifies it across every profile and platform your community runs, on autopilot.

Platforms

Say it once. Everywhere

How you run it

Amplify on autopilot

3 ways in, 1 engine. Every command has a read-only or dry-run form, so you rehearse a run before anything is published.

  • Menu-bar app

    The popover shows what is due, running and about to post. The console covers jobs, content, runs, profiles, analytics, the service and settings.

  • CLI

    The engine behind everything. Each command carries its own help and a worked example, with the preview form first.

    blowhorn post --profile all --dry-run

  • Background service

    A launchd service checks the queue every 10 minutes and runs what is due, with the app closed and nobody watching.

  • Chrome extension

    LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News actions run inside your own signed-in Chrome through the Blowhorn extension. GitHub, Bluesky and Slack go through their APIs.

Broadcast authorized.

Major Blowhorn

Major Blowhorn runs the queue like a drill sergeant. Every approved post is an order, every platform is his platoon, and he barks it out on all of them at once. He barks at the message, never at you.

Trust

Yours to keep

Blowhorn runs as many public voices as you need without handing the keys to anyone else.

  • Your LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News browser sessions stay on your Mac

    Those sessions stay in your own Chrome, where the Blowhorn extension acts for you. Credentials you give Blowhorn for GitHub and Hacker News, and a Slack workspace session you choose to link, are kept in your organization's store on Layer5 Cloud (see the privacy page).

  • Your organization's store, on Layer5 Cloud

    The queue, schedule, profiles, run ledger and platform tokens live in a database scoped to your organization on Layer5 Cloud. Every row carries your organization's id.

  • Hacker News: submit and comment only

    Blowhorn submits stories and posts comments on Hacker News. It never votes.
